Privacy policy

Your time stays yours.

Flip Clock Simple is designed to work without collecting personal information.

Effective August 18, 2026

Information we collect

We do not collect, receive, sell, or share personal information. The app has no account, analytics, advertising, tracking, cloud synchronization, or remote service.

Information stored on your device

The app stores only the settings needed to remember display brightness and timer preferences. It may generate temporary bell sound files in the operating system cache. These files contain no personal information and can be recreated by the app.

This information stays on your device. It is removed when the operating system clears the app’s data or when you uninstall the app.

Network access

Flip Clock Simple does not require an internet connection and does not send app data to us or another service. Apple and Google may independently process store downloads, diagnostics, or account information under their own privacy policies.

Permissions

The app does not request microphone, speech recognition, location, camera, contacts, notification, photo-library, advertising, or tracking permission.

Children

The app is not designed to collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future release changes what information the app processes, this policy and the store privacy declarations will be updated before that release becomes available.
