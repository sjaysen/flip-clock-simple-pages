Simple help, without an account.

Flip Clock Simple is a free offline clock with Pomodoro and meditation timers for Android, iPhone, and iPad.

Using the app

The flip clock opens first. Swipe left for meditation or right for Pomodoro. Use the small dots at the bottom to move directly between screens.

A brightness control appears along the left edge when you touch or hover there. Timer lengths and sound choices are saved on your device.

Timer sound

Check that your device volume is audible and that silent or focus modes are not suppressing sound. Preview a bell from the timer settings before starting a session.

Meditation screen awake

The app asks the operating system to keep the screen awake while meditation is running. Pausing or ending meditation restores normal screen-sleep behavior.

Privacy
